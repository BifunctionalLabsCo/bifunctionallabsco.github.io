# Brand inheritance model

This is the public-safe brand layer available to project ideation. It describes family resemblance without exposing private strategy or forcing every product to look like the studio website.

## Core narrative

Bifunctional pairs technical rigor with human judgment. The work should feel considered, editorial, capable, and direct. Complexity is organized rather than displayed for its own sake.

## Inherited traits

- Clarity over noise.
- Boutique over bloated.
- Structured but warm.
- Technical but human.
- Purposeful typography and spacing.
- Honest language without inflated innovation claims.

## Adaptable dimensions

Each project should derive its own:

- palette and semantic color roles
- display and text typography
- image or illustration language
- component density
- interaction and motion character
- voice within the broader direct, precise tone

## Socratic test

Before choosing aesthetics, answer:

1. What change does the product create?
2. Who needs to trust it?
3. What does the audience already understand?
4. Which Bifunctional traits increase that trust?
5. Which inherited traits would make this product less specific?
6. Which moments deserve delight, restraint, or urgency?

The result should look related to Bifunctional because it shares judgment and standards, not because it copies a homepage.
