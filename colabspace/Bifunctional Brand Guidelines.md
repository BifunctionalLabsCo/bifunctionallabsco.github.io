

# Bifunctional Labs — Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Summary

**Bifunctional Labs** is a Berlin-based, founder-led freelance studio operating at the intersection of product, software, data, design, automation, and emerging technologies.

The studio is compact by design. It is primarily led by Zubin, with Saumya joining selected boutique projects as the design and human-centered research layer. Bifunctional is not positioned as a generic agency, and it is not simply CTO-as-a-Service. It is a small senior unit for founders and teams who need clarity, execution, and creative intelligence without building a full internal team too early.

Bifunctional helps founders move from idea to design, build, automation, and launch.

---

## 2. Core Positioning

### Short Positioning

> A Berlin-based freelance studio guiding founders from idea to design, build, and launch.

### Expanded Positioning

Bifunctional Labs helps early-stage founders, small ventures, and growing teams shape, build, automate, and launch real systems. We combine product thinking, software and data engineering, AI-assisted creative production, and selected design collaboration into compact work packages that move ideas forward quickly.

The studio exists for teams that need senior thinking and practical execution in one place.

### What We Are

### What We Are Not

- Not a generic agency

## 7. Brand Philosophy: Duality

Bifunctional is built on the idea that useful work often happens between two forces.

The name itself implies dual function, synthesis, and productive tension.

### Core Dualities

| Duality | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Strategy / Execution | We think deeply, then build. |
| Structure / Play | Systems need rigor, but creativity needs motion. |
| Product / Story | Good products need both functional clarity and narrative pull. |
| Data / Design | Intelligence must become usable. |
| Human / Machine | AI is a tool for craft, not a replacement for taste. |
| Boutique / Technical | Small team, serious systems. |
| Berlin / Tropical | Urban infrastructure softened by warmth, growth, and life. |

### Duality Principles

- Contradiction is not a flaw. It is a source of design energy.
- Good systems are both rational and emotional.
- Useful technology should feel human without becoming soft.
- Brand and product are not separate worlds.
- Execution is the test of every idea.

### Surface Roles

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|

| Dark Surface Secondary | `#22302b` | oxidized metal, greenhouse framing, dark cards |
| Dark Forest Surface | `#10231d` | deep botanical-industrial background |

Mint and pink are signals, not default surfaces.

Surfaces should feel material:

- steel
- concrete
- paper
- glass
- greenhouse structures
- converted industrial buildings

Brand colors should activate the surface, not overwhelm it.

## 19. Logo Recoloring Guidance

When recoloring the fox logo, use surgical recoloring language.

### Core Rules

- Preserve exact geometry
- Preserve silhouette
- Preserve polygon layout
- Preserve proportions
- Preserve line thickness
- Preserve transparency
- Preserve canvas dimensions
- Color replacement only

### Preferred Logo Recolor Palette

Outlines:

```text
#032B22 Evergreen
```

Primary facet colors:

```text
#40FFB5 Tropical Mint
#FFAFDA Blush Pop
```

Secondary facet colors:

```text
#00634D Emerald Depths
#FF4F81 Bubblegum Pink
```

### Prompt Pattern

```text
IMAGE EDIT TASK

Perform a surgical recoloring of the attached fox logo.

Preserve exactly: geometry, silhouette, polygon layout, proportions, line thickness, transparency, and canvas dimensions.

Do not redesign, reinterpret, simplify, smooth, modernize, enhance, add gradients, add shadows, add lighting, add glow, add texture, add depth, or add 3D effects.

Keep the transparent PNG background unchanged.

Color replacement only.

Use Evergreen (#032B22) for all outlines and structural linework.
Use Tropical Mint (#40FFB5) and Blush Pop (#FFAFDA) as primary facet colors.
Use Emerald Depths (#00634D) and Bubblegum Pink (#FF4F81) as secondary accent facet colors.
Maintain strong contrast between adjacent facets.
Do not introduce any new colors.
Transparent PNG output.
```

## 23. Repo and Agent Guardrails

When modifying Bifunctional files, agents must preserve existing design systems unless explicitly instructed otherwise.

### Do Not

- redesign the site
- reinterpret the brand
- change typography casually
- alter palette values without approval
- change spacing systems
- introduce new visual systems
- add generic SaaS components
- rewrite copy into corporate language
- replace boutique tone with agency tone

### Do

- preserve existing styling
- extend with tokens when needed
- keep backward-compatible aliases
- use brand tokens
- keep changes scoped
- respect the founder-led freelance studio positioning
- document new tokens and patterns clearly
